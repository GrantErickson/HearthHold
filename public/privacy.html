Who we are

HearthHold: Ember Siege is provided by Grant Erickson / MicaPeak Solutions. Questions about this policy: grant@micapeak.net.

Data we collect

The app does not collect, store on servers, share, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

What stays on your device

Game progress and settings are stored only on the device (local storage). That data stays on the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Fonts and network

The Lilita One font is bundled with the app, so the app does not request fonts from the internet. The app makes no external network requests.

Children’s privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
