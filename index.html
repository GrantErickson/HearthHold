Hearthold

Gather. Build. Hold the keep for 100 waves.

  • Move with WASD / arrow keys, or touch and drag anywhere.
  • Walk up to trees and rocks — your hero chops and mines automatically.
  • Stand on the market's SELL pad, then scoop your money from COINS.
  • Stand on a build pad to pay for it. Partial payments are kept.
  • Every kill earns XP. Level up to choose perks (L) and unlock abilities: Q Whirlwind, E War Cry, R Meteor, F Bastion.
  • Bosses every 10 waves drop relics. Build an Academy for research, and watch for chests, merchants and gold rushes.
  • Speed up time with [ and ] or the speed button. Space calls the next wave early.
  • Later on the walls aren't enough: tunnelers break in behind them, thieves raid your coins, scalers climb over and warded foes shrug off towers. Get in there and fight! Step out of red circles.
  • Five acts, 100 waves, ten bosses. Don't let the keep fall!
